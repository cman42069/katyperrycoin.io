Our Mission

StarGirl Coin is more than just a cryptocurrency - it's a movement celebrating women in space exploration, inspired by Katy Perry's cosmic anthems and her support for the groundbreaking all-female space mission. Our goal is to fund and promote women in STEM while creating a vibrant community of holders who believe in breaking barriers.
